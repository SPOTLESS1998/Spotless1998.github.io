Privacy Policy for Autoclipper

Last Updated: August 2026

Autoclipper ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our application to connect with TikTok.

1. Information We Collect

When you authenticate with TikTok through Autoclipper, we receive access to specific information authorized by you, which may include your TikTok profile data and the ability to upload videos on your behalf. We do not collect or store your TikTok login credentials.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the authorized data strictly to provide the Autoclipper service (e.g., publishing your selected content to your TikTok account). We do not use your data for any other unrelated purposes.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal TikTok data with any third parties. Your data is processed securely and only used within the scope of the services you explicitly requested.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

You can revoke Autoclipper's access to your TikTok account at any time via your TikTok settings. Upon revocation, we will no longer have access to your account or data.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer directly.
